v² = 2gR(1 − cosθ)

The drop is R − R cosθ. On a smooth sphere every joule of PE lost becomes KE.
SET THE ANGLE θ
PE LOST BECOMES KE

Measure height from the centre of the sphere. At the top the passenger is R above it and at angle θ he is R cosθ above it, so the drop is Δh = R(1 − cosθ).

The surface is smooth and the normal force is perpendicular to the velocity, so N does no work. Mechanical energy is conserved: ½mv² = mgR(1 − cosθ), giving v² = 2gR(1 − cosθ). Mass cancels.

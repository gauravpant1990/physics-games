WHAT STOPS THE CAR?

Four forces act on a skidding car. Only one of them points backwards along the road.
PICK A ROAD, THEN SKID
ONLY ONE FORCE IS SIDEWAYS

Weight pulls straight down and the road pushes straight up. On a level road those two are equal and opposite, so together they add to nothing and they do no work at all: they are perpendicular to the motion.

That leaves kinetic friction as the only horizontal force. It is not the brake pedal that stops the car, and it is not the weight. It is the road gripping the sliding tyres, and it points backwards because the tyres slide forwards.

🎯 Exam link: Locked wheels means sliding, so use fk = μkN = μkmg, directed opposite to the motion. The deceleration is μkg, with no mass in it.

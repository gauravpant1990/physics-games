HEAVY OR LIGHT, SAME SKID

Load the car up as much as you like. The mark it stops on refuses to move.
SET THE MASS, THEN BRAKE
BOTH SIDES GROW TOGETHER

A heavier car really does carry more kinetic energy, ½mv². But it also presses harder on the road, so friction grows by exactly the same factor: μkmg.

Write ½mv² = μkmg·d and the m cancels from both sides. More energy to remove, but proportionally more force removing it, so the distance is untouched.

🎯 Exam link: d = v²/(2μkg) has no mass in it. A 1000 kg and a 1500 kg car skid identically. Careful: the work done by friction is −½mv², which does depend on mass.

BOAT SHIFT = mL/(M+m)

Still water, no horizontal resistance. A man walks the full length L = 3 m of a 120 kg boat. Set his mass.
SET THE MAN'S MASS, THEN PLAY
ZERO EXTERNAL FORCE PINS THE COM

Horizontally nothing external acts on man + boat, and the system starts at rest, so its COM cannot move. When the man steps one way, the boat must slide the other so the mass-weighted positions still average to the same point.

Let the boat shift x backward. The man walks L relative to the boat, so he moves L − x over the water. Fixing the COM: m(L − x) = Mx, which gives x = mL/(M + m).

Check the limits: a light man barely moves the boat; a man as heavy as the boat splits the walk equally. The buoy shows the water frame, and the marker shows the COM staying on its line throughout.

🎯 Exam link: Man walks L relative to the boat: boat displacement over the water is x = mL/(M + m). It comes from ΔxCOM = 0, so m(L − x) = Mx. With m = 60 kg, M = 120 kg, L = 3 m: x = 1.0 m.

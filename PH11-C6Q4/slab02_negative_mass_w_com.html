TREAT THE CAVITY AS NEGATIVE MASS

A 60 cm slab with a hole touching the rim. Set the hole size and find the balance point.
SET THE HOLE RADIUS
FULL DISC PLUS A NEGATIVE DISC

Uniform density does not mean the mass is spread symmetrically once a piece is missing. The COM follows the material actually present, and the hole side is the lighter side.

Build the slab as a full disc of mass M at the centre plus a disc of mass −m at the hole centre. Since thickness and density are uniform, mass follows area: m/M = (r/R)², which is 1/4 for r = R/2. Then x = [M(0) − m d] / (M − m). For r = R/2 with d = R/2 this gives −R/6: one sixth of the radius, on the solid side, so 10 cm for R = 60 cm.

🎯 Exam link: m/M = (r/R)² and xCOM = −md/(M − m). For r = R/2 touching the rim: R/6 on the solid side.

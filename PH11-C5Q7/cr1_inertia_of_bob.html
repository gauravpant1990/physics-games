THE BOB KEEPS ITS VELOCITY

The support changes speed suddenly. Compare what the support does with what the bob does.
PICK WHAT THE CRANE DOES
NOTHING ACTS ON THE BOB YET

Braking acts on the crane, not on the bob. During the very short braking time the cable is still vertical, so it can exert no horizontal force. By inertia the bob keeps its horizontal velocity v₀, and with the support now at rest it swings forward relative to the crane. Its kinetic energy at that instant is ½mv₀².

Starting from rest is the mirror image: the support moves off while the bob tends to stay at rest, so it lags and appears to swing backward. In neither case is there a real backward or forward force; there is only inertia.

🎯 Exam link: Immediately after braking the bob has horizontal velocity v₀ and kinetic energy ½mv₀². Relative swing comes from the support changing speed, not from a force on the bob.

ONE FORCE, TWO DIRECTIONS

Boots skid on a wet deck. The same friction slows the runners and drives the raft forward.

ACTION AND REACTION ON A DECK

While the boots slide forwards over the deck, friction on the people points backwards and friction on the raft points forwards. Same size, opposite directions, a Newton’s third law pair.

So the runners lose speed while the raft gains it. The instant the two speeds match there is no more sliding, kinetic friction switches off, and they travel on together. Notice the grip changes how fast they lock, never the speed they lock at.

But the deck is only so long. The boots have to skid v₀²/2(apeople+araft) before the speeds meet, and if that is longer than the deck they run off the front into the river.

🎯 Exam link: f = μmg using the people’s weight, since that is what presses on the deck. With m = 200 kg and μ = 0.5 that is 1000 N on each, in opposite directions.
